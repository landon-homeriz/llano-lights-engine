Where should every beam meet? Pick a spot, or tap anywhere on the floor in the view from above.

Two spots, far apart, are all Llano needs: from them it works out how high each GigBAR is, which way it faces and where it stands, so you never measure the bars. Start with the middle of the floor, then a spot near a front corner. A third spot is a double-check.

    Stage setup On on a stage ft high

    Puts the GigBARs on the stage's front corners, 2 ft in from each edge, turned in to the middle of the dance floor. The height is the stand plus the stage: pick the stand and type the stage's height, and the height box fills in. Llano aims "chest height" and "over heads" from it, so get it right before you set the head limits.

    Add

    Drag anything in the view from above to move it, and its corner to size it. The moving lights stay out of table areas and no-beam zones, and inside the head limits: set those on site in Line up my lights (Set how far the heads go), or drag the box here.

    Numbers

    Number the lights the way your cords run: a flip keeps every light where it stands and swaps which one is 1, 2, 3 and so on, so each address moves with its number. Save my layout to keep it.

    Calibrate a head, so the picture and the real beams match

    Pick a head and a dot (they're drawn on the floor below). Move the real beam until it sits on that dot on the floor, then press Set. Start with the center dot, then the near edge and the left edge: from those, each head's aim is worked out. Center alone is often enough; the other two settle which way a head turns.
