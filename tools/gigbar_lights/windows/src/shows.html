How it works

Give it a playlist or a handful of songs. It reads every song's sections (intro, build, drop, breakdown), gives each song a look for its place in the night, and lays the whole show out front to back. Preview its lights here, change any song's look, move songs, add or drop them, and save it by name. On the night the music plays in Mixxx and the lights follow it.

Songs in your Mixxx library get exact sections and make sound in the preview here. Songs only on Spotify get estimated sections, and at the party the lights find their beat by listening.

A show is saved with its songs: add the songs in step 1 first, then Save show keeps them under this name.

1Load the songs

Numbered folders become the parts of the night, in their order: 1 Cocktail Hour, 2 Dinner, 3 Dancing, and a Moments folder for the dances' songs.

From your Drive folder

From your phone: put the song in its part's folder in Drive, then press Get my songs here.

Add a song from this laptop

It is copied into this show's song folder, in that part's folder, so the night build finds it. A song added here stays when you press Get my songs.

Or load them another way

A folder of songs on this laptop, like the wedding folder synced from Drive. Subfolders named Cocktail Hour, Dinner or Dancing set each song's part of the night; files named Artist - Title read best, and tempo, key and year come from the files' tags.

The songs come in by folder name, then file name, A to Z: a folder named 1 - Cocktail Hour comes before 2 - Dinner, and inside each the files run in name order. They go after any songs already in the show.

Your Mixxx playlists and crates, in their order.

A CSV export with artist and title columns works too.

In Spotify: the playlist's ··· menu, Share, Copy link to playlist. It must be public. This reads only the song names and lengths; Spotify doesn't share song structure, so these songs get estimated sections unless they're also in your Mixxx library.

2Name the moments

Pick each moment's song from the list: click a song box and choose. Untick a moment that isn't happening. Times are optional; with them, the night says where you are short on music.

Type it like 6:30. A time that can't be read is left out.

3Listen

Llano Lights reads every song: its tempo, its energy, and where the intro, the builds and the drops are. It starts by itself when songs come in. Then it reads each song's own file for where the dancing really starts and stops, for the Starts at and Ends at in Review.

4Set the mood

Cocktail hour and dinner play a soft wash in this mood, whatever the song. Llano filled in a suggestion; change only what you want different. The same two choices are on the Live screen on the night.

Always, whatever any look says: cocktail hour and dinner never have moving heads on people, spotlights, strobe or laser, and the moving heads run by themselves only in the dances (gently, on the dance floor, in the first dance and the parent dances). Stop heads and Take over on the Live screen are always yours, like any button you press yourself on the night.

5Review

Song order

Suggests an order that flows: easy tempo and key steps, energy that rises and rests, the dances in their places. You see it first; nothing changes until you save it.

Lights

Gives each song a light look for its place in the night, on this laptop, in a second. Looks you chose yourself are kept.

Lights made for this night

You tell Llano about the night (colors, mood) on llano.app and it makes looks only this show has. Needs internet; the show is saved first, and the looks then play with no internet.

Song order

Drag a song to move it; the curve shows the change against the old one. Every song gets a part of the night (it comes from the playlist, crate or folder name, or pick it below) and a reason for its spot. A wedding runs cocktail hour, dinner, then the dancing: the couple's dances, a hype opener everyone knows, waves with breathers, a slow-dance window, and a big sing-along close.

    Songs, in play order

    Change a song's part of the night or its look here. Cocktail hour and dinner songs play a soft wash only, whatever look they have.

    Starts and Ends: where each song's copy for the night begins and finishes. A dance song starts where the dancing starts (a DJ mixes in past a long intro) and finishes where the dancing stops, so the floor never waits. Cocktail hour, dinner and the moments play whole. Tap a song's times to hear it from there and move it. Your own song files are never changed.

    6Build the night

    Build the whole night for Mixxx

    Turns this show into one folder you play from: every song level-matched with a clean start and finish, a silent "hold" for each live moment (toasts, cake, the dances), a few numbered playlists in the order of the night, and a one-page run sheet. Mixxx's Auto DJ plays it; when a moment is over you press the second button at the top of Mixxx's Auto DJ page (Fade Now). Your original files are never changed.

    These wake up once the night is built.

    It builds into your Music folder, under Llano Lights shows, in a folder named for this show. Building the same show again replaces that folder's songs, so close Mixxx first.

      Every song is played whole unless it is listed here. A dance song starts where the dancing starts and finishes where it stops, so the floor never goes quiet between songs. To change where one starts or ends, tap its times in Review (step 5), then build again.

        After the build: close Mixxx (if it asks whether to exit while a deck is playing, click Yes) and press Load Mixxx. It saves a copy of Mixxx's library first, adds this night's songs and numbered playlists, lines up playlist 1 in Auto DJ, sets Auto DJ to Full Track with 2 seconds, and opens Mixxx. On the night, click Auto DJ in Mixxx's list on the left. The first button at the top of that page starts the music (Enable Auto DJ). The second button moves on each time a moment is over (Fade Now). Neither has words on it; the run sheet has a picture.

        If Load Mixxx can't do it: the same thing by hand
        1. Preferences, Library: add the folder above () and let it scan.
        2. Playlists, right click, Import Playlist: import each numbered playlist from that folder.
        3. Auto DJ: pick Full Track and set the box marked sec. beside it to 2. Press "Check Mixxx's Auto DJ" here to confirm.
        4. On the night: right click playlist 1, Add to Auto DJ Queue (replace), then the first button at the top of Mixxx's Auto DJ page (Enable Auto DJ). The run sheet says the rest.
